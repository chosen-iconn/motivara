  • ✓ Role-based access control for teachers, bursars, and principals.

For Academics

CBC-Ready Gradebook & Custom Rubrics

Built from the ground up for the Kenyan Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) as well as 8-4-4 structures. Use custom rubrics, calculate competency scores automatically, and generate comprehensive termly report cards.

  • ✓ Pre-configured CBC evaluation scales (Exceeding, Meeting, Approaching, Below).
  • ✓ Automated class-level and subject-level performance distribution charts.
  • ✓ PDF Report Card generation with custom school crests and signatures.
Operations

QR Check-in & Clash-Free Scheduling

Eliminate double-booked teachers and classroom conflicts. Build smart term timetables automatically while tracking daily classroom attendance through roll-call or ID card check-ins.

  • ✓ Intelligent algorithm prevents teacher and room scheduling overlaps.
  • ✓ Daily attendance registers sync instantly with the parent portal.
  • ✓ Automated absenteeism alerts sent to guardians via SMS.
For Guardians

Transparent Progress & Instant Fee Tracking

Empower parents to stay involved without overwhelming your administrative desk. Guardians can view fee balances, download receipts, monitor academic growth, and message teachers directly.

  • ✓ Real-time fee balance checking and official receipt downloads.
  • ✓ Digital sign-off for homework assignments and discipline notes.
  • ✓ Accessible on any mobile browser or smartphone device.
For Teachers

Intuitive Lesson Planning & Grading Workflows

Free up time spent on administrative paperwork. Teachers can upload lesson plans, distribute digital homework, track behavioral milestones, and submit term grades effortlessly.

  • ✓ Streamlined grading interface designed for speed and accuracy.

For Students

Personalized Dashboard & Learning Resources

Give students ownership of their academic journey. Learners can view their daily timetables, submit assignments online, check upcoming tests, and access revision materials.
